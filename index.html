Overview

A global shift in how work happens

Remote and hybrid work have rapidly transformed the modern workforce. What was once considered a rare privilege became a global norm almost overnight during the COVID-19 pandemic. Before 2020, remote work made up only a small fraction of total working hours. When businesses were forced to adapt, that number increased dramatically, reshaping expectations around where and how work is done. Today, hybrid work—splitting time between home and office—is becoming the dominant model for many industries.

Remote work increased from 5% of paid work hours before the pandemic to nearly 50% in 2020.
